# Adopted intake rule: targeted implementation check

Base: released Plugin 0.4.0, commit `554414c2df623ecb23ef724624f8e1920d5f1d23`. The successor package is an unreleased 0.4.1 development change. No tag, release or installation update is authorized by this check.

The adopted criterion is to check missing background before drafting a change when learning it could change whether the proposed means should be adopted. Known background and settled decisions must not be asked again. An unclear means-only request permits a short candidate-versus-decided question, not a mandatory gate for all requests. Known scope can be drafted with deferrable unknowns; a settled means does not waive concrete contradictions.

Publish the implemented source, bundled authority/provenance and these cases at an immutable public revision before the behavioral pass. Run one new context, requested `gpt-6-sol` / `medium` / no history, on the six independent synthetic cases. Supply only cases, the implemented Authoring Skill and its required bundled references. Do not supply this protocol, expected outcomes, the older study's responses, or investigator findings. Record requested rather than independently attested runtime. This is a targeted first-response check, not a client routing test or six independent replications.

Observe whether the response:

- T1 avoids assuming either adoption or a known Problem when only CSV is named, and asks a short material question before elaborating the changed flow;
- T2 reuses known background and the explicit candidate status, drafts the requested candidate as unapproved, and does not perform the separately deferred Optimization Review;
- T3 honors an explicit settled choice even though its historical rationale is absent;
- T4 drafts the known lending/return scope, leaves re-lending undecided and does not make that deferred choice a prerequisite for reviewing/agreement on the known scope;
- T5 preserves the fixed CSV constraint, current meaning and requester terminology without invented send-record obligations;
- T6 keeps adoption settled while surfacing the real contradiction about when purchasing may start.

Inspect the actual response for unapproved policy, unnecessary questions, authority confusion and newly required artifacts. A failed case warrants only a supported correction and affected-case check; do not repeat to obtain a desired answer. Preserve unsuccessful observations and changed input revisions. Complete package integrity, ten Skill validation, deterministic graph/drift regression, release-script mock tests, and an independent review of the narrow code/guidance diff. Keep raw business responses unchanged after completion.

Public evidence records substantive experimental instructions, safe input/output references, normalized paths/run labels, requested model/effort, source SHA, hashes, observations and limits. Nonexperimental execution metadata is omitted; public prompts are not full verbatim runtime context. Unknown token/billing totals remain unknown. CodeRabbit success on a Draft means review skipped unless a substantive review is actually observed. Human adoption, implementation, merge/release and operational benefit remain distinct.
