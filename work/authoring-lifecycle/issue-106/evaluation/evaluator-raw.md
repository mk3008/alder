## Marginal effect and hypotheses

- **H1 minimal lifecycle — inconclusive as an incremental change.** All six baseline cases already choose the safe substantive outcome, including D/E conflict and C temporal separation. Treatment adds particularly useful old-utterance provenance in A and more explicit labels in B/C/D/E, but does not establish a new ability absent from baseline. Its B wording overstates compatibility. One independent run per cell is not a reliability estimate.
- **H2 materiality filter — inconclusive.** Both arms ignore F's synonymous words/opinions as conflicts and both ask ordinary business questions. No marginal reduction in false-positive conflict topics is observed. The guidance's preference for material business differences is sensible but this experiment does not isolate its effect.
- **H3 coarse topic matching — inconclusive.** D/E are correctly compared on the same decision, B is scoped by cancellation actor and C by effective period, already in both arms. The fixtures do not test a broader set of near-topic but unrelated statements or quantify missed matches. This experiment cannot establish a matching rule's general performance.

**Recommendation:** Decline a permanent wholesale Skill change on these results. The narrowest candidate with a visible marginal benefit is one instruction to link an explicit correction to the *attributed, time-stamped earlier claim* in a concise provenance note, while applying only the corrected rule. Verify that candidate in additional varied runs and inspect cost/verbosity. The baseline's existing safe classifications and treatment's imprecise B wording do not support installing the full five-part guidance now. Neither draft determines customer-approved business meaning; unresolved matters stay for human decision.
