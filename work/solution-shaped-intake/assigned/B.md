Before presenting a new or revised Activity, check Why: it must describe the purpose or expected business condition, not restate Procedure or the requested means. Remove the means mentally; the purpose must still make sense and retain a concrete connection to the affected work. If an existing-business change has no supported purpose or background, ask for the missing Problem or intended condition instead of inventing a Why or treating the means as settled. Do not require a new Problem to justify an explicit new-business decision, reopen agreed meaning, or demand alternatives to a settled external constraint.
