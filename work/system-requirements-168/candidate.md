# Experimental input-and-scope clarification (not adopted)
Apply the current implementation review Skill and full knowledge unchanged, with this additional procedure:
Include supplied System Requirements and relevant project constraints in the review input manifest, pinned to their actual revisions or snapshot digests. Preserve their separate authority and scope; do not merge them into Business Design or create duplicate Checks. Read them with the documented decisions before examining implementation. For the requested change, compare applicable supplied constraints with implementation, DDL, tests and execution evidence. Name the constraint source and evidence for a mismatch, conflict or unverifiable claim; technical nonconformance with an agreed constraint does not by itself require new business approval. If sources conflict or applicability/meaning is unresolved, identify the smallest question and responsible owner. Missing System Requirements means that this portion is unverified, not that no requirements apply; continue independently reviewable work and ask for the missing source only where it affects a concrete decision. Do not invent a general NFR checklist or claim requirements completeness, security certification or production assurance. Business meaning remains in Business Design; System Requirements authoring and completeness remain the product's responsibility.
