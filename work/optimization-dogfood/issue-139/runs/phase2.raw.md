# Alder Optimization Review

Problem: reduce human cognitive burden while preserving required business judgment quality across Alder's actual design/development workflow. Pain level: **High**, supplied explicitly in the review prompt. This is a prompt-only PoC input, not a recorded or confirmed Business Design fact.

The documented work places human judgment in several connected exchanges: **業務設計** receives requirements, reviews correlations and procedures, sends the design and questions to the requester, incorporates decisions and records material resolutions; **検査項目の設計** separately explains observable expectations, receives review results and updates Checks until agreement; **業務改善レビュー**, when requested, presents alternatives for human adoption decisions. Adjacent **システム設計** gathers technical conditions, and **実装** returns unresolved business meaning to design. The adoption guide adds independent post-implementation review and a separate follow-up.

AI already supports Business Design drafting, Check drafting and updates, improvement proposals, implementation and review. Check-level feedback is already normally applied by AI. Routine reversible technical choices are already delegated, and people already answer only unresolved business decisions in implementation review. Those existing responsibilities are not new optimization candidates.

The request establishes a burden and High pain, but does not identify which exchanges cause it, how often they occur or how judgment quality is assessed. Context reconstruction between exchanges is therefore a **burden hypothesis**, not an observed source fact. The Business Design's description also does not independently establish that the requester has confirmed this as current operational practice.

## Candidate 1 — Coordinate human review around the same business passage across Design and Checks

1. **Candidate:** Change the requester-facing exchange between 業務設計 and 検査項目の設計 so that a reviewer can follow one business passage from its agreed meaning to its observable Check expectations, with the relevant source passage, exceptions, downstream guarantee and distinct questions available together. Keep the maintained Business Design and Check Items; do not introduce another permanent specification.
2. **Relation to the Problem:** If people currently reconstruct the same business situation when moving from design agreement to Check review, this arrangement could remove repeated navigation and recollection while retaining both judgments. It addresses the existing explanation/review exchanges, not an invented report-production Activity.
3. **Approach:** Simplify the exchange and presentation; eliminate repeated reconstruction where it is genuinely redundant; preserve meaning review. AI prepares and maintains the presentation as assistance. AI drafting itself is already present and supplies no additional benefit claim here.
4. **Scope:** **Keep.** This changes the coordination of two existing in-scope Activities and their requester exchanges, without extending Alder into product development or transferring decision authority.
5. **Expected benefit:** Less movement between documents and less need to remember how a Check follows from a business passage. The benefit is conditional and unmeasured. The presentation must retain relationships across Activities; a convenient local excerpt cannot substitute for reading the applicable whole design.
6. **Difficulty:** **Medium.** The designer and requester must coordinate the two review exchanges and keep source revisions and distinct review states understandable. No external party, service or contract change is established. Difficulty arises from changing review practice, not from the size of a possible viewer implementation.
7. **Affected parties / affected business:** 業務設計者 and 依頼者 in 業務設計 and 検査項目の設計; システム開発者 receives the same agreed handoff with revisions and confirmed Check IDs.
8. **Existing Business meaning and constraints to preserve:** Business Design remains the human-maintainable SSOT in the requester's language. Design agreement and human Check expectation review remain required and distinguishable. Check drafting/review follows agreement on the relevant Business Design; a combined presentation does not authorize premature Check approval. Preserve independent expectations, Check IDs, mappings and review states. A Check that exposes unresolved meaning returns to Business Design for resolution and agreement first. Test evidence remains separate from human confirmation, and new Test mappings are not a pre-handoff gate. People retain authority over meaning, responsibility, exceptions and guarantees.
9. **Assumptions / Unknowns:** Whether repeated reconstruction actually contributes materially to this High pain; whether seeing source and expectations together reduces effort or instead obscures the wider workflow; whether the current review medium can support this without a duplicate artifact. The adoption guide already supplies a two-layer Check view, so improving that view alone is not the proposed responsibility/exchange change.
10. **Questions people must decide or verify:** For a representative actual review, does moving from design agreement to Check explanation make the requester reconstruct already-discussed work? Would a connected presentation let them make both judgments with fewer navigation steps while still detecting incorrect expectations and cross-Activity effects? The designer and requester decide whether to change this exchange after checking that concrete case. They need not reconsider the already-established SSOT or approval rules.
11. **Confidence:** **Medium** that the proposal is grounded in documented exchanges; **Low** that it reduces burden without affecting judgment quality, pending actual-work evidence.

## Search boundary and current-work confirmation

I considered removing human Check confirmation entirely. That would remove one review exchange, but would also change the required human confirmation of independently observable expectations. Derivation confidence and passing Tests cannot replace this judgment under the supplied design. This branch is not retained as a candidate. No evidence here supports compensating for that loss through another control.

The retained proposal keeps human decisions and changes how their evidence is presented. Presentation coordination can vary without changing who approves meaning; abolishing a required approval is a different responsibility decision and is excluded under current constraints. I stopped before adding batching schedules, external services, workload assumptions or additional approval gates because the inputs do not establish their operational basis. High pain justifies investigating a meaningful exchange change, but does not establish its benefit or justify inventing further candidates. This is not a complete search or an optimum claim.

The bounded Business Design expressly excludes the general development process after handoff, post-implementation Test execution/review/deployment, and Alder research, maintenance and release. The adoption guide and README describe the development loop at a general level, but do not supply a target product's actual responsibilities and handoffs. **Focused current-work confirmation:** which actual post-handoff exchange creates the burden, who performs it, what input/output and judgment are involved, and how does it return to design? Obtain that current-work description before proposing development-loop changes. Do not invent Activities to fill the gap.

One candidate is returned; none is selected or approved. If people adopt a change, update and re-agree Business Design first, then align affected Checks, Decisions, Tests and implementation. No source files or GitHub records were modified.
