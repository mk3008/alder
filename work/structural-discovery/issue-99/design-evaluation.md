## Disposition

**Discovery may proceed as a read-only investigation.** The drafts and dialogue provide enough explicit evidence to probe a structural cross-business problem: one numbered set can serve residents or courses; a resident's Friday-to-Monday promise spans a Saturday course; the course records type and count on Wednesday but chooses numbered sets on Friday; the school course removes four sets beginning Friday noon. The records, actors, times, and open decisions are sufficiently specified to test whether the business can keep commitments consistent. **V3 is not an agreed operating rule or a safe implementation specification.** It leaves allocation, revision, and notification unresolved and contains one current-practice assertion unsupported by the customer's answer. Discovery should cite the raw customer dialogue and mark hypotheses separately from facts.

## Fidelity and unknowns

V1 stays appropriately sparse after the initial brief and asks relevant questions. V2 captures the customer's answers on registered residents, Friday-to-Monday loan periods, signatures, Wednesday course count, Friday numbering, distinct use-before checks, and maintenance reinstatement. V3 correctly distinguishes current operations from next month's school course and retains many open decisions. Its school notice owner is marked unknown, as the customer did not name one. There is no spoken basis for a priority policy, a fixed total supply, a numeric loss estimate, new dedicated stock, or a settled school-course intake procedure; the drafts generally avoid these assertions. The one material fidelity error is promoting the window's *ability to read* the course schedule into a *routine check*.

Some unknowns in V3 are rightly open. In particular, the role card contains later school-course detail not delivered in the transcript; treating that withheld information as if the designer had heard it would invalidate this review. Other unknowns need sharper framing: distinguish an as-is missing cross-check from a proposed control, a resident promise from its provisional numbered allocation, and a returned physical set from one cleared by maintenance. Do not make a settled policy from an unanswered design question.

## Recommended design repair before sign-off

1. Correct the current window procedure to match the spoken checks, and mark any comparison against course demand as a proposed question or future step.
2. Add a shared, time-bounded correlation of each loan promise, numbered assignment, ordinary-course count and later numbered selection, and school-course occurrence/packing. Specify where each fact is recorded only after customer confirmation.
3. Obtain the operator's decisions for priorities, sufficiency checks, conflict escalation, reassignment approval, and affected resident/lecturer/school notifications; capture what happens when maintenance removes a previously assigned set.
4. Define the school's planning and handoff facts, including four-set demand before notice, Friday-noon physical movement through Saturday-evening return, the role/evidence for each transfer, and the maintenance release. Mark unanswered details as pending rather than adopting the withheld role-card answers.

These repairs are conditions for treating a future revision as a usable business agreement. They do not prevent the independent Discovery work from starting on the documented evidence.
