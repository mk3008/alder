# Issue #108 — independent Fresh evaluation (initial assessment)

**Verdict: Adopt with narrower wording.** A bounded, optional structural inquiry is justified when an established current-state relation spans multiple activities, records, roles, times or results. It must permit zero candidates and send any candidate to human confirmation. The evidence does not justify treating an unconfirmed correlation in a draft as a discovered improvement, treating an observation as a Problem/Pain, or adding an optimization mode, required step, metrics store or plugin.

## A's nine observations: what each actually establishes

1. **Shared supply and different commitments (§1): genuine opportunity to investigate.** One finite numbered pool serves resident intervals, ordinary class type/count and school packing; the v4 loan allocation and class planning/preparation show their timing and records differ. The *lack of a settled common allocation point* is an inference from unresolved priority/check rules, not proof that staff never coordinate or have suffered clashes. Reconstruct an actual week and confirm policy with owners.
2. **Count to numbered asset (§2): grounded structural dependency, largely a facet of §1.** Wednesday counts, Friday afternoon individual selection, Friday noon school packing and a resident's earlier numbered promise make a useful commitment-state question. A proposed tentative/committed taxonomy is not current practice.
3. **Tag, record and physical readiness (§3): primarily an acknowledged authoring/Quality Review question.** V4 maintenance and unresolved item 4 already ask how return time, tag and physical confirmation align. The existence of distinct evidence is established; disagreement, unnecessary work and any benefit from a new reconciliation event are not.
4. **Return to inspection (§4): primarily an acknowledged handoff gap.** Loan and class returns lead to inspection; v4 unresolved items 3 and 7 explicitly leave transfer and school receipt unconfirmed. This is useful to complete Business Design, but cannot be credited as a discovery of a current operational defect.
5. **School custody chain (§5): the Friday-to-Saturday physical custody is established, but the proposed manifest addresses unknown handoffs.** V4 says four sets and protective gear are packed Friday noon and tools return Saturday evening, while carrier, recipients, numbers and protective-gear return remain unanswered. The time-bounded shared-stock implication supports §1; a manifest is a speculative design answer.
6. **School notice (§6): authoring gap.** The notification activity says `(none)` input and unknown sender, route and confirmation; v4 unresolved item 5 already asks those questions. An absent design input does not establish that actual staff send notices without a plan.
7. **Records (§7): mixed, mostly generic correlation question.** The loan ledger, handover record, class schedule, inspection sheet and maintenance record have distinct roles, and v4 expressly leaves the assigned-number record destination unknown. Relating asset number and time could support §1, but a shared record/link is not independently validated as an improvement or evidence that existing records cannot already be traced.
8. **Class completion versus readiness (§8): useful separation, primarily confirmation of criteria.** The established decisions differ; v4 unresolved items 2, 4 and 7 already ask their detailed criteria. This preserves negative controls but does not demonstrate a new current-state opportunity. Do not infer redundancy of resident signature, instructor setup check or maintenance functional check.
9. **Milestone timeline (§9): grounded cross-service test of §1, not a ninth independent finding.** Wednesday, two Friday moments and Saturday uses allow a real calendar/inventory test; v4 does not say ordinary and school classes coincide or that noon-to-afternoon reuse is possible. Until dates, stock and turnaround are known, no shortfall is established.

Thus §§1, 2 and 9 substantiate one central, conditional structural question. §5 supplies a relevant custody fact and §§3–4, 6–8 mainly expose acknowledged incomplete design relations or preserve important distinctions. The favorable `issue-99/oracle-evaluation.md` assessment of oracle recall does not certify operational pain, authoring completion, stakeholder agreement or nine separate improvement opportunities.

## Minimal documentation wording and gate

Suggested optional wording for the existing Discovery guidance (no new mode):

> Business Design の現行業務の意味と主要な Activity 間の相関が確認されている場合、複数の Activity／Object／Role／When／Result にまたがる、現行構造を再検討する価値があり得る関係を、根拠・成立している関係・仮説・未確認事項・人間への質問を分けて示してよい。候補がなければ0件とする。未確認の手順、権限、記録間の相関は先に Business Design の確認へ戻し、改善候補に数えない。Problem／Pain や効果を推定せず、候補の採否は業務当事者が判断する。目的の異なる確認を重複として統合しない。

**Gate:** Existing Authoring/Quality Review must first establish source fidelity, material current-state meaning and the relevant cross-activity correlation. If any relation needed for a proposed observation is pending, return a plain Business Design confirmation question and count zero improvement candidates for that relation. A passed authoring gate enables inquiry only; human confirmation decides whether an opportunity is a Problem worth acting on. This fits B's pass and later rejection, C's zero, D's return to authoring, and A's valuable central hypothesis while reserving its unfinished correlations for design completion.

**Limits:** Four recorded synthetic cases, with one C and one D Fresh run, cannot estimate discovery accuracy, general zero rate, real-world pain, benefit, or cost. The requested `gpt-6-sol`/medium/no-fork settings and pinned source tree are recorded, not independently verified as effective; C/D read logs report their inputs, but a shared filesystem cannot prove access isolation. This evaluation is read-only with respect to all source designs, fixtures, raw runs and previous assessments.
