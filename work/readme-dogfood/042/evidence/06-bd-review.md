### Material business questions

1. **Return discrepancy and completion condition.** Lines 23 and 146–152 say the comparison source is unknown and explicitly ask what happens on a mismatch. Yet lines 159–161 describe receipt as confirmed and the set awaiting inspection. If a number or accessory fails to match, the normal end state cannot be evaluated: is receipt withheld, provisionally recorded, or accepted under another person's decision? Confirm what information is authoritative for each comparison, who decides mismatches, and whether/when the return timestamp and receipt confirmation are recorded. Reflect the chosen normal versus exception outcome in the corresponding fields. This is a business rule question, not a proposed answer.

2. **Receipt-to-maintenance handoff.** Lines 24 and 157 explicitly leave notification and physical handoff to maintenance unresolved, while line 161 says the returned set is awaiting maintenance inspection. The present Result does not establish how the maintenance role knows which set is waiting or who holds it after receipt. Confirm the responsible role and minimum transfer/notification state needed to make “点検待ち” true. Inspection itself and its completion remain outside the stated Scope (lines 27, 33).

3. **Loan handover evidence and actor.** Lines 22, 95, 105–113 leave open who signs, who creates the handover record, and who confirms it. Treating the signed record as an Input is understandable if someone else supplies it, but the current text cannot show whether signature/record creation occurs within this Activity and who is accountable. The draft already flags the Scope dependence (line 17).
