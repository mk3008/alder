TR-01–04 cover successful acceptance, recording, inspection-wait state and continued unavailability; TR-05–08 separately cover nonacceptance, no timestamp write, no inspection-wait transition and return of 要確認; TR-09 covers preserving the first timestamp on reprocessing. **Clarify one coverage boundary before approval.** The design says acceptance occurs *only* when both number and accessories “match” (lines 9, 121–122), but its explicit rejection cases name number mismatch and accessory shortage (lines 11, 128). TR-05–08 inherit those narrower cases. Extra accessories or a different accessory with no numerical shortage therefore have no explicit expected exception path, while the Check draft itself acknowledges extra accessories as undefined. Ask the responsible requester whether “付属品が一致” means exact identity and quantity of the loaned set, and what result applies to extras or substitutions. If those cases are to be rejected, revise and human-confirm Business Design first, then update the affected Checks with the same IDs or new IDs as warranted. Do not assume an answer from the word “一致” alone.

**Minor wording correction for a future authorized update.** TR-08's title says “返せる”, which may read as an optional capability although the expected result and Business Design specify that the result *is* returned to the counter. Consider an invariant title such as “不一致・不足の結果を窓口へ要確認として返す”; retain ID and expected result. This is editorial, not a new business decision. TR-03 and TR-04 should stay separate: inspection-wait status and unavailability can be reviewed independently.

**Existing open questions, no repeated decision now.** BD-Q01 (actual receiver versus counter role) remains important before any role/authority Check is added. BD-Q02/03 cover notification/custody and steps after 要確認; neither should be silently promoted to pass/fail Checks. Physical handoff was expressly deferred and should not be re-asked for this scope. The whole Business Design is explicitly an unapproved draft, so this review cannot mark Checks confirmed or describe the design as ready for handoff.
