# Phase 9 preregistration — placement judgment with and without a component label

## Question and boundary

Does the frozen Phase 7 physical-placement judgment, without an architecture/design label, lead an agent to express feature-only, subsystem-shared, and application-wide structural dependency ranges through the frozen Phase 8 longitudinal sequence; or does adding `Use Package by Component.` provide a practical structural difference?

This is not an architecture ranking, Scope-First candidate process, or normative rule process. Phase 3 remains `INVALID`; Phase 4 remains exploratory; Phase 5 and 7 remain research advice; Phase 6 remains `COMPLETE`; Phase 8 evidence/status remains unchanged.

Primary evidence is physical structural topology, ordered semantic scope, structural scope, then enforced visibility. Node ESM export/import visibility is secondary only.

## Exact reuse

The initial fixture, frozen Stage 1–5 behavioral tests, business packets, public entry-point requirement, and common implementation/refactoring prompts are byte-for-byte copies of the Phase 8 frozen inputs. Phase 9 starts from the copied initial fixture, never a Phase 8 final arm state. The Phase 8 source trees, diffs, inventories, reviews, and prior results are excluded from worker prompts and working instructions.

## Arms

| arm | exact directive |
| --- | --- |
| A `placement-judgment-only` | exact bytes of `prompts/arm-a.txt` |
| B `package-by-component-plus-placement` | exact bytes of `prompts/arm-b.txt` |

The sole intended arm difference is the presence of `Use Package by Component.` in Arm B. No other label, architecture explanation, topology example, or Scope-First wording is supplied to workers.

## Procedure

Each arm begins from the same copied fixture and proceeds Stage 1 through 5 by inheriting only its own prior refactoring snapshot. At every stage: a separately requested fresh Terra/medium implementation task, frozen behavioral verification, snapshot; then a separately requested fresh Terra/medium refactoring task, frozen behavioral verification, snapshot. Frozen test/task/prompt edits are protocol deviations: retain the source evidence, mechanically restore the exact frozen artifact before continuation, and record the action.

Each candidate worker may inspect only its assigned same-arm stage directory, its copied frozen current test, the current business packet, common execution prompt, and its directive; it must not inspect Phase 8 material or the other arm. A fresh Sol/medium read-only reviewer inspects preserved Phase 9 evidence only after its evidence commit.

Actual model/version/session ID, timestamps, and per-run Git SHA are recorded `unverified` unless directly exposed. No composite score or winner determination is emitted.
