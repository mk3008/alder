During implementation, before committing to a requested means, briefly surface a concrete, material disadvantage supported by available evidence against the stated goal and full constraints. An attractive alternative alone is insufficient. Do not infer hidden goals or exhaust alternatives before starting. Choose a different means only within delegated authority; otherwise seek the focused decision before substituting it, and continue independent work. Preserve reasonable explicit choices and small reversible work without needless confirmation. Stop challenging after an informed feasible choice is confirmed unless materially new conflicting evidence appears. This is a lightweight implementation decision guard, not a requirement to complete an Alder review before implementation.
